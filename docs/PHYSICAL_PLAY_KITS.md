# VEC Physical Play & Discovery Showcase

The public `/play` page is a controlled showcase for approved physical activity kits, learning games and interactive kits. It communicates that the current range is in development/manufacturing and does not imply that products are ready to purchase.

## Managing entries

Use the **Physical play & discovery kit** document type in the Content Studio. Add only approved public names, product photography/mock-ups and short descriptions. Set `stage` accurately. A public price is displayed only when the stage is `available` and a price has been supplied. Keep manufacturing drawings, supplier quotes, cost sheets and unreleased designs outside the public CMS.

## Current public behaviour

When no approved kit entries exist, the page displays three category-level teasers: activity kits, learning games and interactive kits. These are collection categories, not invented product names or products for sale. All cards lead to the general enquiry page; no checkout or payment flow is implied.
