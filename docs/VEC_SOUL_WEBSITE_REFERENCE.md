# VEC Soul — Website Reference

The whole VEC website is guided by **The Soul of VEC** rather than presenting it as a long corporate manifesto.

Used as design DNA:
- Experience before explanation.
- Story + Discovery + Emotion + Beauty.
- The world feels alive beyond the visitor's immediate interaction.
- The three hearts: Child, Parent, Teacher.
- The Discovery Contract: See → Feel → Think → Discover → Live.
- “Knowledge is not the destination. It is the reward for curiosity.”
- The Grey Ripple is an intermittent force that can make questions or answers difficult to access.
- Discovery Spark and Anvaya Journal recur as canon VEC world elements.
- The Ancient Door and Question Chamber are story experiences, not conventional FAQ widgets.
